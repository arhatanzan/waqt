Calculation Methodology

This timetable integrates strict astronomical precision with traditional Shia jurisprudential rules (Ihtiyat) and historical documentation.

  • Tark-e-Saher: -19° Solar Depression Angle, followed by a strict 10-minute subtraction buffer.
  • Maghrib: -4.5° Solar Depression Angle (eastern redness passing zenith), followed by a 5-minute subtraction buffer.
  • Tareeq (Nahas/Saad): Derived from authentic traditions of Imam Jafar al-Sadiq (A.S.) and *Tuhfat al-Awam*. The 3rd, 5th, 13th, 16th, 21st, 24th, and 25th of every lunar month are standard *Nahas* (Heavy). Crucially, the 1st of Muharram, Safar, Rabi al-Awwal, and Rabi-us-Sani are explicitly recognized as *Nahas Akbar* (Major Inauspicious).
